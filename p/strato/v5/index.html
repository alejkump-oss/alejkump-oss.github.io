Strato Apartment

Strato Apartment je družinsko voden apartma v Kočevju, ki ga gostita Barbara in Rok. Leta 2025 v celoti prenovljen in posodobljen, meri 74 m² in sprejme do 5 oseb. Kočevsko jezero je približno 270 m od apartmaja, približno 400 m do središča Kočevja.Strato Apartment is a family-run apartment in Kočevje hosted by Barbara and Rok. Fully renovated and modernised in 2025, it measures 74 m² and sleeps up to 5 guests. Lake Kočevje is about 270 m from the apartment, about 400 m to Kočevje town centre.

2 spalnici (velika zakonska postelja in zakonska postelja) ter raztegljiv kavč. Zasebno parkirišče in dvorišče na posesti. Govorimo slovensko, angleško, hrvaško in srbsko.2 bedrooms (a king-size and a double bed) plus a sofa bed. Private parking and courtyard on the property. We speak Slovenian, English, Croatian and Serbian.
