O apartmajuAbout the apartment

Prostor, ki diha.A place that breathes.

Strato Apartment meri 74 m² in sprejme do 5 oseb. 2 spalnici (velika zakonska postelja in zakonska postelja) ter raztegljiv kavč. Kočevsko jezero je približno 270 m od apartmaja, približno 400 m do središča Kočevja.Strato Apartment measures 74 m² and sleeps up to 5 guests. 2 bedrooms (a king-size and a double bed) plus a sofa bed. Lake Kočevje is about 270 m from the apartment, about 400 m to Kočevje town centre.

Dnevna soba s TV, klavirjem, kitaro in tolkali, otroškimi igračami in družabnimi igrami; neposreden izhod na teraso.Living room with TV, piano, guitar and percussion, children's toys and board games; direct access to the terrace.

Zasebno parkirišče in dvorišče na posesti. Prijava od 15:00 (osebni sprejem ali samostojna prijava s kodo). Odjava do 11:00.Private parking and courtyard on the property. Check-in from 15:00 (in person or self check-in with a code). Check-out until 11:00.
