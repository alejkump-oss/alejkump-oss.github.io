Kočevje

Strato Apartment je 74 m² velik apartma v Kočevju za do 5 oseb. 2 spalnici (velika zakonska postelja in zakonska postelja) ter raztegljiv kavč. Kočevsko jezero je približno 270 m od apartmaja, približno 400 m do središča Kočevja. Leta 2025 v celoti prenovljen in posodobljen. Gostitelja sta Barbara in Rok.Strato Apartment is a 74 m² apartment in Kočevje for up to 5 guests. 2 bedrooms (a king-size and a double bed) plus a sofa bed. Lake Kočevje is about 270 m from the apartment, about 400 m to Kočevje town centre. Fully renovated and modernised in 2025. Your hosts are Barbara and Rok.
