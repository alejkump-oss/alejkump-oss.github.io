Strato Apartment

Strato Apartment se nahaja v Kočevju, na naslovu Trata VI 11. Kočevsko jezero je približno 270 m od apartmaja, približno 400 m do središča Kočevja. Zasebno parkirišče in dvorišče na posesti. Leta 2025 v celoti prenovljen in posodobljen, meri 74 m² in sprejme do 5 oseb.Strato Apartment is in Kočevje, at Trata VI 11. Lake Kočevje is about 270 m from the apartment, about 400 m to Kočevje town centre. Private parking and courtyard on the property. Fully renovated and modernised in 2025, it measures 74 m² and sleeps up to 5 guests.

2 spalnici (velika zakonska postelja in zakonska postelja) ter raztegljiv kavč. Dnevna soba s TV, klavirjem, kitaro in tolkali, otroškimi igračami in družabnimi igrami; neposreden izhod na teraso. Popolnoma opremljena kuhinja: kavni aparati, kuhalnik vode, blender, pomivalni stroj, indukcijska plošča, pečica, hladilnik, mikrovalovna pečica, osnovna živila. Kopalnica s tušem, pralni stroj, sušilec za lase, kozmetično ogledalo. Ogrevanje s toplotno črpalko, klimatska naprava. Terasa s senčnikom, vrt, prostor za žar. Brezplačen WiFi, otroška posteljica, zasebni vhod, copati, dežniki, knjiga gostov. Govorimo slovensko, angleško, hrvaško in srbsko.2 bedrooms (a king-size and a double bed) plus a sofa bed. Living room with TV, piano, guitar and percussion, children's toys and board games; direct access to the terrace. Fully equipped kitchen: coffee machines, kettle, blender, dishwasher, induction hob, oven, fridge, microwave, basic supplies. Bathroom with shower, washing machine, hair dryer, cosmetic mirror. Heat-pump heating and air conditioning. Terrace with sunshade, garden, barbecue area. Free WiFi, baby cot, private entrance, slippers, umbrellas, guest book. We speak Slovenian, English, Croatian and Serbian.
